PROGRAM 03 • SPACE TECHNOLOGY

Space Systems

Explore satellites, spacecraft, propulsion and technologies designed for missions beyond Earth.

PROGRAM OVERVIEW

Explore Technology Beyond Earth

Space Systems introduces students to the technologies that make space exploration possible, from satellites and spacecraft to communications and propulsion.

What You Will Study

  • Satellite systems
  • Spacecraft design
  • Orbital mechanics
  • Space communications
  • Rocket propulsion
  • Space mission planning
  • Remote sensing
  • Space science fundamentals

Practical Experience

Students can work on satellite concepts, space mission projects, simulations and engineering design challenges.

Possible Career Directions

  • Space Systems Engineer
  • Satellite Engineer
  • Mission Systems
  • Space Research
  • Remote Sensing
  • Space Technology
