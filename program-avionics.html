PROGRAM 04 • ELECTRONICS

Avionics & Aircraft Systems

Explore the electronics, computers, navigation and control systems that make modern aircraft possible.

PROGRAM OVERVIEW

The Technology Inside The Aircraft

Avionics combines electronics, computing and aviation. Students learn how aircraft communicate, navigate and control complex systems.

What You Will Study

  • Aircraft electronics
  • Navigation systems
  • Communication systems
  • Flight control systems
  • Aircraft instrumentation
  • Embedded systems
  • Digital electronics
  • Aircraft electrical systems

Practical Experience

Students work with electronics projects, control systems, simulations and aircraft technology demonstrations.

Possible Career Directions

  • Avionics Engineer
  • Aircraft Systems Engineer
  • Embedded Systems Engineer
  • Flight Control Engineer
  • Aircraft Electronics Technician
  • Aviation Technology Specialist
