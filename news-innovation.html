AeroNova Introduces New Aviation Technology Lab

May 28, 2026

AeroNova has introduced a new technology laboratory designed to give students more opportunities to explore aviation and aerospace engineering.

A New Space for Innovation

The laboratory provides an environment where students can work on engineering projects, explore aircraft systems and develop new ideas.

The facility is designed to support practical learning and encourage students to turn engineering concepts into working projects.
