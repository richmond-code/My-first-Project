Students Complete Aerospace Engineering Challenge

June 05, 2026

AeroNova students recently completed an aerospace engineering challenge designed to test their technical knowledge, creativity and teamwork.

Engineering Through Teamwork

Students worked together to develop solutions to engineering problems while applying concepts from their aerospace and engineering studies.

The challenge gave students an opportunity to develop problem-solving skills and gain practical experience working as an engineering team.
