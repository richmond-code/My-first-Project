AeroNova Students Begin Flight Training

June 18, 2026

AeroNova students have begun a new stage of practical aviation training focused on understanding aircraft, flight preparation and aviation safety.

Practical Aviation Experience

Students are introduced to important aviation systems and procedures while developing the technical knowledge required for future aviation careers.

The training combines classroom learning with practical activities designed to help students understand how modern aircraft operate.
