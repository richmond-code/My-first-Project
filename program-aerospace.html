PROGRAM 01 • AEROSPACE

Aerospace Engineering

Learn how aircraft, rockets and spacecraft are designed, developed and tested to push the boundaries of flight.

PROGRAM OVERVIEW

Design & Build The Future Of Flight

Aerospace Engineering focuses on the science and technology behind atmospheric and space flight vehicles. Students explore advanced aerodynamics, lightweight structures, propulsion systems and orbital mechanics.

What You Will Study

  • Aerodynamics and flight principles
  • Aircraft structures and composite materials
  • Propulsion and jet engine systems
  • Rocket propulsion and launch dynamics
  • Flight mechanics and control systems
  • Spacecraft design and orbital systems
  • Computational simulation and CFD
  • Aerospace manufacturing and safety standards

Practical Experience

Students gain hands-on training through wind tunnel testing, advanced CAD design projects, propulsion test simulations, and collaborative aerospace engineering labs.

Possible Career Directions

  • Aerospace Engineer
  • Aircraft Design Specialist
  • Propulsion Systems Engineer
  • Aerodynamics Analyst
  • Flight Test Engineer
  • Spacecraft Systems Engineer
  • Defense & Aviation Consultant
