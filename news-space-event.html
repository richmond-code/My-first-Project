AeroNova Hosts Space Technology Event

June 12, 2026

AeroNova students recently took part in a space technology event exploring the science and engineering behind modern space exploration.

Exploring Space Systems

Students learned about satellites, spacecraft, propulsion systems and the technologies that allow missions to operate beyond Earth.

The event provided an opportunity for students to connect their classroom knowledge with real-world aerospace challenges.
